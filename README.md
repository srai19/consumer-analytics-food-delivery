# Consumer Analytics: Revenue, Retention & Customer Experience

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce transaction data to understand **what drives revenue, profitability, and customer satisfaction**.
Using **SQL and Python**, the analysis focuses on customer retention, time-based trends, discount effectiveness, user behavior, and delivery experience to derive **actionable business insights**.

The goal is to demonstrate **business-first analytics thinking**, not just technical execution.

---

## Business Questions Addressed

1. **Revenue Baseline**

   * What is the overall scale of the business in terms of orders, customers, revenue, and AOV?

2. **Retention vs Profitability**

   * Are returning customers more valuable than new customers?

3. **Month-wise Revenue Trends**

   * How is business performance changing over time?
   * Are there signs of growth, stagnation, or seasonality?

4. **Discount Impact Analysis**

   * Do discounts drive incremental revenue or erode profitability?

5. **Customer Experience Analysis**

   * How does delivery time affect customer satisfaction?

6. **Behaviour Analysis**

   * Does higher user engagement (session duration, pages viewed) translate into higher spending?


## Tools & Technologies

* **SQL** – business logic, aggregations, segmentation
* **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)** – data validation, analysis, visualization
* **GitHub** – project structuring and version control


## Key Insights

* **Retention matters**: Returning customers show stronger engagement, but need targeted incentives rather than blanket discounts.
* **Time trends matter**: Month-wise analysis highlights revenue fluctuations and growth phases that static analysis would miss.
* **Discounts underperform**: Discounted orders generate lower revenue and AOV, suggesting margin erosion rather than growth.
* **Delivery experience is critical**: Longer delivery times lead to noticeable drops in customer ratings.
* **Engagement ≠ Spending**: Short, decisive sessions generate higher order values, while longer sessions may indicate decision friction.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Shift focus from broad discounting to **targeted, behavior-based promotions**.
* Invest in **logistics efficiency** to reduce long delivery times and improve satisfaction.
* Improve **product discoverability and UX** to convert long browsing sessions into higher-value purchases.
* Prioritize **retention-led growth** over volume-driven acquisition.
